WHAT I LEARNED

Seeing Our School Differently

Creating this portfolio helped me notice details around our school that I usually overlook. Simple places such as classrooms, pathways, gardens, and activity areas became more interesting when I observed their lighting, colors, lines, and patterns.

I learned that composition, camera angles, focus, and natural lighting can make a photograph clearer and more meaningful. I also practiced cropping, resizing, adjusting colors, and writing captions for my photographs.

Most importantly, I learned to take photographs responsibly. I should ask permission before taking or publishing identifiable pictures of other people. This portfolio shows my creativity while respecting the privacy of our school community.
